What to know about this forecast⌄

This MVP uses explainable rules, without a language model. The coefficients are pilot assumptions, not measured accuracy. Missing CSV dates are not treated as zero sales. Stockouts can make recorded sales underestimate demand.

    Stock on hand comes from the latest CSV entry. shelf_life_days means days of shelf life remaining as of today. The weekly plan assumes the same shelf life for new batches. Discounts do not guarantee sales. Waste risk is not assessed for products with more than 14 days remaining.

    Which columns do I need?date,product,qty,price,stock,shelf_life_days

    date — YYYY-MM-DD; qty — units sold per day; price — price in KZT; stock — stock on hand; shelf_life_days — remaining shelf life in days. Stock and shelf life come from the latest entry for each product. Zero means expired.

    Use one row per product per date. Include days without sales with qty=0. Quantities must be whole units (items, packs or portions). Commas and semicolons are accepted.

    Your data is available only in this browser session and expires after 2 hours of inactivity or a server restart.
    EVERY NUMBER, EXPLAINED

    No black box.

    This MVP uses transparent rules. The written summary comes from the calculation, without an external AI service or API key.

    1. Start with baseline demand.Average the last four matching weekdays within 56 days. If none are available, use the last 14 records.
    2. Add the local context.Nauryz, New Year, Kurban Ait and March 8: +30% for flour, meat and sweet treats. The 5th and 20th: +10%. Office areas on weekends: −40%; university areas: −25%, with another −20% in summer.
    3. Check the weather.Below +5°C: tea +20%, ice cream −30%. At +28°C or above: tea −20%, ice cream +30%. In summer: tea −10%, ice cream +10%. Factors are multiplied. If weather is unavailable, no weather adjustment is applied.
    4. Allow a small buffer.Add a 5% buffer for products with up to 2 days remaining, or 10% for others, rounded up. Subtract usable stock. The weekly plan tracks carryover and batch expiry.
    All coefficients are demo assumptions. A pilot should validate and tune them against real sales. This site never places orders automatically.
